About Me
======
I have an immense love for all things biology , both professionally and on a personal level.
Ober the course of my existence , I have bounced between many research projects , some bearing fruit others not so much.
Early in my career I worked in a neuroscience lab , primarily investigating the effects Translocator Protein (18 kDa) ligands 
on a behavioral and systemic level. During this time I became very familiar with tools such as, electroencephalography (EEG) , 
behavioral assays, and fluorescence microscopy. After my training in academia concluded, I ventured out to see the industry side of things.
My journey took me to biomanufacturing and GMP processing, where I learned how to separate immune cells via immunomagnetic separation, analyze 
them for purity via flow cytometry , and cryopreserve them. Now, with the growing usage of new technology in biology , I am planning to venture into the field
of Bioinformatics. So wish me Luck!
